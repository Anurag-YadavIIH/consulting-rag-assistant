Meridian Health: outpatient scheduling pilot review

The outpatient scheduling pilot ran for twelve weeks across three clinics. It aimed to cut the average wait for a first appointment from 19 days to 12 days, and reached 15 days.

The pilot missed its target mainly because referral letters still arrived by fax at two of the three clinics, so the new booking tool started each case a day or more late. Staffing gaps on Fridays added further delay.

Next steps: move the two remaining clinics to electronic referrals before a second pilot, and add Friday cover. For questions, the pilot lead is Dana Whitfield (dana.whitfield@meridian-health.example).
