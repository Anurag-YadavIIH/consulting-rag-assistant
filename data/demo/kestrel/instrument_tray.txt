Kestrel Medical: instrument tray evaluation

The new modular instrument tray was tested in two sterile processing departments. Its full sterilisation cycle takes 38 minutes, compared with 52 minutes for the current tray, because the open lattice design dries faster.

Technicians rated the tray easier to inspect. One department reported that the smallest clips were harder to count, and asked for a labelled insert.

The evaluation recommends adopting the tray with the labelled insert. Evaluation coordinator: Sam Okafor (sam.okafor@kestrel-medical.example).
