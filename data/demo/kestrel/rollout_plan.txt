Kestrel Medical: rollout plan

The rollout is planned in three waves over nine months. The first wave covers Northgate General, Riverside Community Hospital and St. Aldric Medical Centre, chosen because their sterile processing teams took part in the evaluation.

Each wave starts with two days of on-site training and a four-week review. The second and third waves follow only if the first wave keeps tray turnaround under 45 minutes.

Rollout questions go to the programme office at rollout@kestrel-medical.example.
